The Color Wheel

Every color on this page starts life as a mix of just three primary colors: red, yellow, and blue. Combine them in different amounts and you get everything from a fiery orange to a deep indigo.

Warm colors like red, orange, and yellow tend to feel energetic and bold, while cool colors like green, blue, and purple feel calm and relaxed. Pink and white round out the set with softer, lighter tones.
