Trees are very important for people, animals, and nature. They can grow very tall and live for many years. Trees have roots, trunks, branches, and leaves. Their roots take water and nutrients from the soil, while their leaves use sunlight to help the trees make food. Trees produce oxygen that people and animals need to breathe, and they also help clean the air. Many birds build their nests in trees, and trees provide homes and food for many animals. They give us shade on hot and sunny days. Some trees produce fruits such as apples, oranges, and cherries. People use wood from trees to make furniture and paper. Trees also help prevent soil from being washed away by rain. Planting more trees can help protect our environment. We should take care of trees because they are very important for life on Earth.
